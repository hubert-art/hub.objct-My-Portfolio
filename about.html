About Me

A developer shaped by curiosity, design, and product thinking.

I am a Software Engineering student at United States International University - Africa, learning how to turn ambitious ideas into reliable digital products.

Software engineering attracted me because it combines logic, creativity, and impact. I love the moment where a rough idea becomes something people can touch, use, and enjoy.

My goal is to become the kind of engineer who can move from strategy to interface to backend architecture with care. I am motivated by elegant systems, useful design, and the belief that great software should feel human.

My technical curiosity began with hands-on Arduino and electronics experimentation around the age of 13. It now extends to Raspberry Pi, practical electricity knowledge, and the way hardware and software can work together to solve real problems.

I also explore 3D modelling, animation, and Blender as part of a wider creative-technology practice. These interests help me move between software, hardware, and visual thinking with the same care for useful, human-centred outcomes.

Flocon Energie is a practical example of how these interests can meet: a project grounded in electrical services and shaped through software, engineering thinking, and clear digital communication.
